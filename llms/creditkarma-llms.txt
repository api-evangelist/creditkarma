# Credit Karma

> Credit Karma is a US personal-finance platform (a subsidiary of Intuit) offering free credit scores and reports, credit and identity monitoring, personalized financial-product recommendations, and Credit Karma Money banking. It does not operate a public developer API program; this profile captures its public engineering, security, and identity surface for the API Evangelist network.
